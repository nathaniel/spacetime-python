Spacetime

Coordinates use units where the speed of light is 1. The diagram shows position on the horizontal axis and time upward. Clocks accumulate proper time along their piecewise-constant-velocity worldlines; flashes travel at the speed of light.

Use the Coordinates menu to inspect synchronized positions. The reference-frame menu applies a Lorentz transform without changing the underlying scenario.
